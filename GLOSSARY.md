# Surprise App

Personal scrollable appreciation experience built to learn animation, UI/UX, and performance with production-grade standards.

## Language

**Scroll Journey**:
The single scrollable experience from tulip hero through finale.
_Avoid_: tap journey, tap flow, screens

**Tulip Hero**:
The gated opening running seed drop through bloom on black gradient with staged haptics.
_Avoid_: hero image, static flower, stock photo

**Journey Section**:
One vertical block of the scroll rendering one unit of the story (hero, one beat, finale).
_Avoid_: screen, page, step

**Story Beat**:
One cherished moment told as an ordered list of small blocks, revealed bit by bit on scroll.
_Avoid_: letter block, photo dump, paragraph

**Beat Block**:
One small unit inside a beat (text reveal, photo pop/parallax, drawn line, glow spacer).
_Avoid_: full layout, screen section

**Snippet**:
One or two lines of text inside a beat, never a full letter.
_Avoid_: letter, paragraph, caption dump

**Haptic Cue**:
Staged ticks for grow and pop plus light on reveal and medium at bloom and finale, each fired once.
_Avoid_: vibration on every scroll, sound effect

**Finale**:
The closing section with a short note and hearts bloom after the last beat.
_Avoid_: summary, credits roll
